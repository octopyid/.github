# OctopyID

**OctopyID** is a software development organization based in Indonesia.

At present, the organization is maintained by a single developer. However, it is structured and documented with the intention of long-term growth, scalability, and the possibility of evolving into a formal company in the future.

---

## 🏢 About OctopyID

OctopyID serves as a central organization for developing, maintaining, and publishing software projects with a strong emphasis on:

* Code quality and maintainability
* Clear architecture and structure
* Practical, real-world solutions
* Long-term sustainability

While currently operated individually, all repositories are managed using standards that are suitable for team collaboration and organizational expansion.

---

## 🔧 Scope of Work

Projects within this organization may include:

* Backend services and APIs
* Internal and public developer tools
* Frameworks, libraries, and reusable packages
* Infrastructure and DevOps configurations
* Research, prototypes, and technical explorations

Repositories may be categorized as:

* **Stable**: Suitable for production use
* **Experimental**: Used for research or early-stage development
* **Work in Progress (WIP)**: Under active development

---

## 🧱 Technology Stack

Technology choices vary by project, but commonly include:

### Backend

* **PHP**: Laravel, RESTful APIs
* **Node.js**: JavaScript / TypeScript-based services

### Frontend

* **JavaScript / TypeScript**
* **Tailwind CSS**, Alpine.js

### DevOps & Infrastructure

* **Docker**, Docker Compose
* **Linux** (Debian-based systems)

### Testing

* **Pest**, **PHPUnit** (PHP ecosystem)
* **Jest**, **Vitest** (Node.js / TypeScript ecosystem)

---

## 📂 Engineering Principles

Across all repositories, the following principles are applied:

* Clean, readable, and consistent code
* Clear separation of concerns
* English naming for code and documentation
* Documentation that explains intent and design decisions
* Preference for maintainability over short-term convenience

## 🤝 Contributions

At this stage, OctopyID primarily functions as an internal development organization.

External contributions may be considered on a case-by-case basis through:

* Issues and discussions

Contribution guidelines may be formalized as the organization grows.
